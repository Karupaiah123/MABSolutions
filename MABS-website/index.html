About MABS

A consultancy built on technical expertise and client-focused service

MABS – Measuretech Analysis and Building Solutions is a professional construction consultancy firm dedicated to delivering professional, innovative, reliable and cost-effective solutions for residential, commercial, industrial and infrastructure projects.

We combine technical expertise, industry knowledge and client-focused service to provide end-to-end consultancy solutions — supporting clients from concept to completion, and ensuring quality, sustainability and value at every stage of the project lifecycle.
