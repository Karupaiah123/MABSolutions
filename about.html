Company Overview

Who we are

MABS – Measuretech Analysis and Building Solutions is a professional construction consultancy firm dedicated to delivering professional, innovative, reliable and cost-effective solutions for residential, commercial, industrial and infrastructure projects.

We combine technical expertise, industry knowledge and client-focused service to provide end-to-end consultancy solutions — supporting clients from concept to completion, and ensuring quality, sustainability and value at every stage of the project lifecycle.

Our Vision

To become one of the most trusted and respected construction consultancy firms

By delivering innovative, sustainable and value-driven engineering solutions to every client we serve.

Our Mission

Deliver professional consultancy services with integrity and excellence.

Help clients optimize cost, time and project performance.

Our Approach

Technical rigour, plain communication

Every engagement begins with understanding the client's objectives, budget and timeline. From there, MABS applies structured planning, cost control and coordinated engineering input to keep projects on track — with transparent reporting at every stage.

Whether the assignment calls for full project management or focused support in one discipline — quantity surveying, scheduling, contracts or engineering — the same standard of professionalism and accountability applies.
